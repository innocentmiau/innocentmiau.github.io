01  About

A bit more background

Most of my projects start as solo experiments meant to refine one specific mechanic, and some grow into full collaborative builds or game jam entries. Outside of shipping games themselves, I spend a lot of time on the tooling around them - editor scripts, asset pipelines, and shaders for Unity and Blender that make the next project faster to build.
